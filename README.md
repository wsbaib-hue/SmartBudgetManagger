A project for managing Budget.
Part 2b readme
what is SmartBudget Manager?
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook designed to help users record monthly income and expenses, categorize spending, calculate a monthly balance, view expenses by category, and notice budget overruns when the relevant warning is configured.
workbook worksheets
•	Dashboard — summarizes total income, total expenses, monthly balance, and charts or indicators available in the workbook.
•	Transactions — the main worksheet for entering income and expense records.
•	Categories — lists predefined categories such as Food, Transport, Rent, Health, and Education, and may contain budget settings.
requirements
•	Microsoft Excel 2016, Excel 2019, Excel 2021, Microsoft 365, or compatible spreadsheet software.
•	The SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx workbook file.
•	Basic skills in selecting cells, entering dates and numbers, and saving a workbook.
quick start
1.	Open SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx in Excel or a compatible application.
2.	Save a working copy with a new filename before entering personal data.
3.	Open Transactions and select the first empty row in the transaction table.
4.	Enter the transaction date and a short description.
5.	Choose the transaction type and category, where applicable.
6.	Enter the amount in the appropriate Income or Expense column, then press Enter.
7.	Open Dashboard and review the totals and monthly balance.
8.	Save your changes with Ctrl+S.
important notes
Do not overwrite formula cells or delete worksheet headings. Review categories and budget settings before relying on a warning. If a total appears incorrect, check dates, amounts, categories, and the relevant formulas. Keep a backup of the original workbook.
